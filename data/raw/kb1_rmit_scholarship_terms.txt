These Terms and Conditions are aligned with the Higher Education Support (Commonwealth Scholarships) Guidelines 2025 and the Higher Education Support Act 2003 Cth.

1. Introduction and Scope
1.1 These Terms and Conditions outline the eligibility requirements, benefits, obligations and administration of Higher Degree by Research (HDR) scholarships at RMIT University.
1.2 These Terms and Conditions should be read in conjunction with the University policies and procedures documented in the Policy Register.
1.3 RMIT University reserves the right to adjust these Terms and Conditions at any time and will notify all scholarship holders in writing of any adjustments.

2. Types of scholarship
2.1 Research scholarships at RMIT are awarded through a fair transparent merit-based selection process having regard to the University's commitment to equity and diversity. There are two types of research scholarships: stipend scholarships and fees offset scholarships.

| Scholarship type | Funded by | Stipend | Fees offset | Allowance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RTP Stipend Scholarship (RSS) | Commonwealth Government | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| RMIT Research Stipend Scholarship (RRSS) | RMIT | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Vice-Chancellor's PhD Scholarship (VCPS) | RMIT | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| College/School Based Research Stipend Scholarship | RMIT | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| RTP Fees Offset Scholarship (Domestic) | Commonwealth Government | No | Yes | No |
| RMIT International Tuition Fee Scholarship (RITFS) | RMIT | No | Yes | No |

2.2 The Federal Government's Research Training Program (RTP) provides funding for stipend scholarships (with associated allowances) and fees offset scholarships. RTP Stipend Scholarships provide a living allowance (stipend) paid to awardees on a fortnightly basis. RTP Fees Offset Scholarships cover the tuition fees for the program and do not directly provide funds to candidates. The duration of Fees Offset scholarship is four years for full-time PhD candidates and two years for Masters by Research candidates.
2.3 RMIT University stipend scholarships, fees offset scholarships and allowances are provided to research candidates based on these Terms and Conditions.
2.4 These Terms and Conditions apply to all research scholarships awarded through RMIT's research scholarship rounds including scholarships funded by Colleges, Schools and external sources where this document has been nominated as the applicable Terms and Conditions.

3. Eligibility Requirements
3.1 To be eligible for a scholarship under these Terms and Conditions a student must meet the eligibility requirements of the Australian Commonwealth and RMIT's eligibility requirements.
3.2 To meet the Commonwealth eligibility requirements an applicant must be eligible to enrol or currently be enrolled in an HDR program at RMIT and not be receiving any other Commonwealth award or scholarship designed to offset HDR tuition fees.
3.3 To meet RMIT's eligibility requirements an applicant must satisfy the scholarship-specific restrictions set out below:
3.3.1 For RTP Stipend Scholarships the applicant must not have previously held a Commonwealth-funded postgraduate research scholarship (unless it was terminated within six months of payments commencing) and must not have previously held an RMIT-funded scholarship that was voluntarily relinquished.
3.3.2 For the Vice-Chancellor's PhD Scholarship (VCPS) the applicant must not have undertaken any prior higher degree by research candidature in the last three years.
3.3.3 If applying for a research masters the applicant must not already hold a research doctorate, a research masters or an equivalent qualification.
3.3.4 If undertaking a research doctorate a scholarship applicant must not hold a research doctorate or an equivalent research qualification unless they are an Australian and New Zealand citizen undertaking a second PhD in which case they are eligible for the RTP Fee Offset scholarship only.
3.3.5 Currently enrolled candidates and candidates transferring to RMIT from another institution must not have consumed more than 3.0 Equivalent Full-time Study Load (EFTSL) in a PhD program or 1.0 EFTSL in a Master's by Research program prior to the scholarship commencement.

4. Application and Selection Process
4.1 Applications for research scholarships must be submitted in the form approved and by the date determined by RMIT.
4.2 RMIT will only offer a research scholarship to a candidate as a result of an application lodged as part of a competitive selection process.
4.3 Stipend and International Fees Offset Scholarships are awarded through a competitive selection process that ranks applicants according to academic achievement, research outputs and relevant professional experience. Priority is given to applicants who have achieved a high distinction (80% or above at RMIT) in the final two years of their qualifying program(s).

5. Conditions of Award
5.1 Scholarship awardees must enrol and commence their RMIT program in the calendar year the scholarship was awarded unless RMIT determine otherwise at its discretion. Commencement cannot be deferred into the following calendar year.
5.2 All scholarship awardees are expected to maintain their enrolment in their HDR program at RMIT and to make satisfactory academic progress throughout their program.
5.3 All scholarship awardees are expected to be enrolled on a full-time basis. A part-time stipend scholarship may be approved only where a candidate can demonstrate that they meet the grounds set out in clause 5.4. International candidates must additionally meet any conditions attached to their student visa.
5.4 The grounds for approving a part-time stipend scholarship are:
5.4.1 having primary carer responsibilities for a pre-school child;
5.4.2 having primary carer responsibilities for school-aged children as a sole parent with limited access to outside support;
5.4.3 having primary carer responsibilities for an invalid or disabled spouse, child or parent; or
5.4.4 having a medical condition that limits the capacity to undertake full-time study (supported by medical certification from a qualified healthcare practitioner).
5.5 In considering applications for a part-time scholarship RMIT must be satisfied that the applicant's circumstances are such that it would not be reasonable to expect full-time study. Work commitments cannot be accepted as grounds for converting a stipend scholarship to part-time.
5.6 A candidate who holds a part-time stipend scholarship may revert to full-time study at any time with RMIT's permission. RMIT may also require part-time scholarship holders to convert to full-time if the exceptional circumstances that gave rise to part-time eligibility no longer apply.
5.7 Stipends for part-time scholarships are not tax exempt.
5.8 RTP Stipend holders may undertake paid part-time employment provided it does not impede progress towards completion of their research degree. Work commitments cannot be accepted as grounds for an extension of the scholarship duration.

6. Scholarship Duration
6.1 The duration of a full-time stipend scholarship is three years and six months for a candidate undertaking research doctorate studies and two years for a candidate undertaking a research masters. Part-time duration is seven years for research doctorate studies and four years for research masters studies.
6.2 The duration of a stipend scholarship will be reduced by any periods of study undertaken towards the degree prior to the commencement of scholarship.
6.3 The duration of a stipend scholarship will be increased in accordance with relevant Leave Entitlements.
6.4 RMIT may in its absolute discretion and on a case-by-case basis grant candidates a single stipend extension of up to 3 months only. Candidates who have not successfully completed the third Milestone are ineligible to apply for a stipend extension.
6.5 If a part-time stipend scholarship is converted to full-time then the period of time that the candidate has been in receipt of the stipend scholarship immediately prior to the conversion will be halved for the purpose of determining the remaining duration of the stipend scholarship.
6.6 If a full-time research scholarship is converted to part-time research scholarship then the period of time that the candidate has been in receipt of the stipend scholarship immediately prior to the conversion will be doubled for the purpose of determining the remaining duration of the stipend scholarship.
6.7 If a candidate submits their research for examination prior to the end of the stipend scholarship the stipend will cease from the day on which the candidate submits their thesis.

7. Scholarship Payments and Value
7.1 RMIT will pay the full value of a stipend scholarship unless the scholarship is reduced or ceased due to circumstances including but not limited to the candidate commencing after the approved start date, suspending the scholarship or the scholarship being terminated.
7.2 RMIT must make fortnightly payments directly to candidates.
7.3 Scholarship recipients share responsibility for ensuring they are not overpaid. If RMIT determines that a candidate has been overpaid the candidate must repay the full amount within 30 days of being requested to do so.
7.4 Annual stipend rates are available on the RMIT Research Scholarships page published by the Commonwealth Government.
7.5 Part-time stipends will be paid at half the full-time rate.

8. Allowances and Leave Entitlements
8.1 Scholarship allowances are provided for RMIT centrally funded and Commonwealth Government funded (RTP) stipend scholarships.
8.2 The relocation allowance covers the cost of relocating the recipient, their spouse and dependents to a new place of residence where this is necessary to enable the candidate to undertake the HDR. Reimbursement is available up to AU$515 per adult and AU$255 per dependent child subject to a maximum total reimbursement of AU$1,540 per candidate.
8.3 For scholarship holders relocating within Victoria the relocation allowance is only payable where the candidate is relocating to Melbourne from a location at least 200km outside the Melbourne metropolitan area.
8.4 Claims for the relocation allowance must be submitted no later than six months after arrival at RMIT via the RMIT Connect Portal. Applicants who already reside in Melbourne or who relocated prior to receiving their scholarship offer are not eligible for the relocation allowance.
8.5 VCPS recipients are eligible to receive a research support allowance of up to $5,000 per annum for the duration of the scholarship.
8.6 Candidates in receipt of a full-time stipend scholarship are entitled to 20 working days of paid recreation leave per year. Candidates in receipt of part-time stipend scholarship are entitled to 10 working days per year.
8.7 Candidates on a stipend scholarship are entitled to 10 working days of paid personal leave per year (5 working days for part-time candidates).
8.8 Where accrued personal leave is insufficient candidates may access up to 60 additional working days of paid personal leave over the life of their stipend scholarship provided a medical certificate is supplied.
8.9 Parental leave is available to Primary Carers who have completed 12 months of study while in receipt of their stipend and are entitled to a minimum of 20 weeks of paid parental leave for each child.
8.10 Candidates in receipt of a full-time stipend scholarship are entitled to 10 working days of paid family and domestic violence leave per year. Candidates in receipt of a part-time stipend scholarship are entitled to 5 working days per year.

9. Ongoing Eligibility and Administration
9.1 A scholarship holder must promptly notify RMIT of any change in circumstances that may affect their eligibility to continue receiving scholarship payments including any proposed change to student load (full-time or part-time) or failure to maintain enrolment at the University.
9.2 Candidates may apply to RMIT for a leave of absence while in receipt of a scholarship. The approval for leave will be at the discretion of RMIT.
9.3 If the proposed leave is to commence after the first six months of tenure and is of less than twelve months' duration a College or School may at its discretion approve the leave.
9.4 If the proposed leave of absence is to commence within the first six months of tenure or if the accumulated period of suspension(s) is of more than twelve months' duration the candidate must follow the process outlined at the Enrolment Leave and changes to candidature webpage.
9.5 The end date of the research scholarship will be extended for any periods of approved leave of absence.
9.6 If a candidate becomes ineligible for their stipend scholarship while still enrolled they may apply to suspend their stipend payments. Suspension of stipend payments does not extend the duration of the stipend scholarship.

10. Transferring Scholarships
10.1 The continuation of any stipend scholarship in relation to a program transfer is at the discretion of the receiving university and will always be subject to that university having sufficient funding and its own applicable policies.
10.2 By approving a transfer to or within RMIT by an RTP Scholarship recipient RMIT commits to providing the equivalent RTP Scholarship at RMIT rates for the remaining standard duration of the scholarship in the new program.
10.3 Where a student transfers their stipend scholarship to RMIT and has accessed leave entitlements during the same calendar year at their previous institution RMIT may subtract the amount of leave accessed by the student from the leave entitlements it provides the student for that calendar year.
10.4 RMIT-funded stipend scholarships cannot be transferred to another university.
10.5 Candidates may request a transfer of their RTP stipend scholarship to another university however RTP scholarship support provided through RMIT will cease upon cancellation of enrolment at RMIT.
10.6 A student's period of support will not be increased beyond the standard scholarship duration limits as a result of an approved transfer.
10.7 Candidates who upgrade from a research master's to a research doctorate degree will have their scholarship extended to the standard duration of a PhD scholarship. Conversely candidates who downgrade from a research doctorate to a research masters will have their scholarship reduced to the standard duration of a research masters scholarship.

11. Termination, Misconduct and Review
11.1 RMIT will terminate a research scholarship in any of the following circumstances:
11.1.1 the candidate ceases to meet the eligibility criteria specified in these Terms and Conditions;
11.1.2 the candidate ceases to be enrolled full-time and approval for part-time study has not been obtained;
11.1.3 RMIT determines that the candidate is not carrying out their research in accordance with these Terms and Conditions;
11.1.4 the candidate has committed serious misconduct as defined in the Student Conduct Policy including the provision of false or misleading information.
11.2 If a research scholarship is terminated it cannot be re-activated unless the termination occurred in error.
11.3 If RMIT knows or has reason to believe that a candidate has provided false or misleading information in relation to their research scholarship RMIT must immediately re-assess the candidate's entitlement to the scholarship.
11.4 Current scholarship holders have the right to request a review of any decision made by RMIT in relation to their scholarship.

12. Acknowledgement of Commonwealth Contribution
12.1 Recipients of RTP Stipend and Fees Offset Scholarships must acknowledge the Commonwealth's contribution in a prominent place in any published or produced material relating to the research project carried out under the scholarship.
12.2 The acknowledgement must include: "This research was supported by the Commonwealth through an Australian Government Research Training Program Scholarship."

13. Further Information
These Terms and Conditions are based on the Higher Education Support (Commonwealth Scholarships) Guidelines 2025. All scholarships funded by the Australian Government and RMIT are administered by the School of Graduate Research.

Glossary
Act: The Higher Education Support Act 2003 Cth.
Candidate: A person who is enrolled in a Higher Degree by Research (HDR) program at RMIT University.
Domestic student: Has the same meaning as in the Higher Education Support Act 2003 Cth.
Equivalent Full-Time Study Load (EFTSL): The measure used by RMIT University and the Australian Government to calculate a candidate's study load and determine eligibility, scholarship duration and other enrolment-related entitlements.
Fees Offset Scholarship: A scholarship that covers tuition fees.
Full-time study: Enrolment in an HDR program on a full-time basis in accordance with the University's enrolment requirements.
HDR: Higher Degree by Research.
International student: A student who is not a domestic student as defined in the Higher Education Support Act 2003 Cth.
Masters by Research: A Level 9 Masters Degree (Research) as defined in the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF).
Part-time scholarship: A stipend scholarship held while a candidate is enrolled on a part-time basis with the approval of RMIT University.
Research Doctorate: A Level 10 Doctoral Degree (Research) as defined in the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF).
Research Scholarships: Scholarships administered by the School of Graduate Research to support HDR candidates.
RTP: The Australian Government Research Training Program.
Scholarship holder: A candidate in receipt of a scholarship.
Stipend Scholarship: A scholarship that provides a living allowance to support a candidate while undertaking an HDR program.
Suspension: In relation to a Stipend Scholarship, an approved period during which scholarship payments cease while the scholarship holder remains enrolled in the HDR program.
